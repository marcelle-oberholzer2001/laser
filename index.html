      Prepare & aftercare

      Before and after your session

      Before

      • Avoid the sun and tanning beds for at least 2 weeks before. Wear a high-SPF sunblock on the area.
      • No waxing, plucking or epilating for 4 weeks before your session. Shaving is fine.
      • Shave the area the day before your appointment.
      • Stop self-tanner and bleaching creams for 2 weeks before.
      • Arrive with clean skin, with no make-up, deodorant, creams or perfume on the area.
      • Tell us about any medication you take, because some make skin sensitive to light.

      After

      • Some redness or warmth is normal. A cool compress or aloe vera helps.
      • Skip hot baths, saunas, steam and hard exercise for 24–48 hours.
      • Stay out of the sun and use SPF 50 on the treated area.
      • Don't wax or pluck between sessions. Shave if you need to.
      • Treated hairs can take 1–3 weeks to shed. That's part of the process.
      • Keep to your session schedule for the best results.

      Questions

      Good to know

      Does it hurt?

      Most clients describe it as a warm snap, like an elastic band against the skin. It's quick, and most people find it much more comfortable than waxing.

      How many sessions will I need?

      Hair grows in cycles and the laser can only treat hair that is actively growing, so a course of sessions is needed. The number depends on the area, your hair and your skin. We'll give you an estimate at your consultation.

      How far apart are sessions?

      Usually every 4–8 weeks, depending on the area. Facial hair grows faster and is treated more often than legs or back.

      Can I shave between sessions?

      Yes, shaving is fine. Just don't wax, pluck or epilate, because the laser needs the hair root to be there.

      Who shouldn't have laser?

      We don't treat during pregnancy, over tattoos, or over freshly tanned or sunburnt skin. Some medications and conditions need a check first. We go through all of this at your consultation.

      What if I need to cancel?

      Please give at least 24 hours' notice so the slot can go to someone else. You can reschedule through Fresha.
